# Master Skill: Glwadys Creative Portfolio

## Identity & Core Concept: "The Creative Layers"
Glwadys Dalleau is a high-level Social Media Manager, Content Creator, and Brand Strategist based in Marseille, France (~5 years freelance experience).
The portfolio tells a dual story:
- **FRONT**: What the public sees (captivating visual content, high-retention Reels, aspirational Stories, seamless branding).
- **BEHIND**: What structures the attention (rigorous calendar planning, moodboards, brand positioning, copywriting, market research, content pillars).

## Narrative Progression & Flow
1. **Intro & Depth Emergence**: Dark backdrop, microscopic metadata, razor-thin horizontal line, initial media planes emerging in Z-depth (far to near).
2. **Hero & Kinetic Typography**: Asymmetrical headline ("I TURN BRANDS INTO STORIES PEOPLE REMEMBER"), layered in Z with Glwadys portrait and content cards.
3. **Identity & The Deconstructed Grid**: Portrait expands, deconstructs into precise grid cells moving on X/Y/Z, transforming directly into social presence cards.
4. **Social World & The Signature Dive**: Floating multi-depth social cards (Posts, Reels, Stories) with the Front/Behind interactive layer switch. A central story enters a sleek 3D smartphone frame, followed by a cinematic camera dive *through* the phone screen.
5. **Inside Content (3D Editorial Gallery)**:
   - Phase X: Vertical scroll drives horizontal gallery travel.
   - Phase Y: Camera traverses vertically into curated media compositions.
   - Phase Z: Spatial depth progression through an infinite digital gallery.
6. **Chaos to Order (Brand Communication)**: Media collages converge into structured typography, color swatches, tone of voice, and brand architecture.
7. **Strategy Grid**: Transition from creative chaos to ivory strategic order. Pinned horizontal timeline revealing content pillars, editorial calendars, and brand positioning.
8. **About**: Visual breathing room. Serene portrait with four progressive kinetic statements.
9. **Freelance Journey (2021-2026)**: Horizontal milestone timeline passing in front of and behind massive typography.
10. **Selected Work Constellation**: Spatial 3D constellation between major case studies:
    - **Yuna Bijoux** (Brest, 2022-2023): Jewelry storytelling, content reach, 3D phone showcase with media bursting in X/Y/Z.
    - **Marseille Girls Club** (Marseille, 2025-2026): Digital scrapbook aesthetic, event snapshots, community engagement.
    - **Le Comptoir de Mathilde** (Marseille, 2024-Present): Refined luxury product merchandising, warm tones, slow macro depth.
11. **Services & Experience Stacking**: Large typographic services with hover previews, followed by Y-axis stacking cards pushing previous milestones into Z-depth with IPAC Bachelor education note.
12. **Final Callback & Contact**: All world objects drift into a harmonious final spatial galaxy around the call to action ("LET'S CREATE SOMETHING PEOPLE REMEMBER").

## Technical & Motion Guidelines
- **Director**: GSAP ScrollTrigger orchestrating a Master Timeline.
- **Stage**: Persistent React Three Fiber `<Canvas>` maintaining state and camera coordinates without remounts.
- **Surface**: Semantic HTML/CSS with next-intl (FR / EN / KO) and full accessibility.
- **Tiers**:
  - `HIGH`: Full WebGL, camera dive, GLSL displacement shaders, cursor reveal.
  - `MEDIUM`: Clamped DPR, simplified planes, reduced geometry, mobile gesture optimized.
  - `SAFE`: Zero WebGL, clean CSS hardware-accelerated transforms and GSAP fallback.
- **Strict Anti-Slop Rules**:
  - No purple AI gradients or glowing spheres.
  - No generic SaaS cards or bento grids.
  - No arbitrary particle clouds.
  - No elastic or spring overshoot curves; use refined editorial easings (`power3.out`, `expo.out`, `sine.inOut`).
  - Strict real-data fidelity: never invent fake client metrics or stats.
