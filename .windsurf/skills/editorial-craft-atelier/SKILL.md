# Editorial Craft & Anti-Slop Atelier Skill

## Overview
Guidelines for high-end luxury editorial digital design. Rejects generic SaaS cards, neon purple gradients, AI glow spheres, random floating cubes, and template layouts.

## Core Tenets
1. **Asymmetric Composition & Hierarchy**: Every screen has intentional weight, counter-balancing heavy display typography with delicate metadata, fine lines, and generous negative space.
2. **Layered Depth (Front / Behind)**: Editorial layers reveal the craft: the glossy public content layer (Reels, campaigns, stories) and the strategic backing (moodboards, calendars, positioning, editorial briefs).
3. **Typography as Structure**: Strong contrast between large condensed serif/sans headlines and ultra-clean mono or grotesque micro-labels.
4. **Restraint in Motion**: Every motion must communicate intention (e.g., cell decomposition represents feed curation; camera dive represents entering the strategy).
5. **No AI Clichés**: No generic bento grids, no meaningless floating 3D spheres, no neon cyberpunk highlights. Color palette is rooted in refined editorial tones: Obsidian, Warm Cream, Champagne Gold, Deep Espresso, and Ivory.
