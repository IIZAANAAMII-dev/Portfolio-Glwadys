## Mentor's original contributions to Sonnet storyboard

### 1. Film ribbon must be LUXURY, not a CSS filmstrip demo
Perforations are marginal details: 10–14px mobile, low-contrast champagne alpha <= .45. Let photography dominate >=65% of the primary composition, not six equally-sized thumbnails. Use ONE meaningful large hero frame, smaller side frames only as glimpses of memory. Film unspools in a physical gesture: a frame passes across camera, settles at exact destination, next act CONTINUES from same crop. Insert a stable hold after each transformation (15–25% of scrub segment); avoid constant panning / blur and concurrent unrelated movements. Year follows the image, never floating aimlessly.

### 2. Distinct chapter palettes / emotional rhythm
Act00 darkroom black with one tactile hero. Work near-black with rare champagne glints. Work→Journey wine-to-wood physical matte, NOT a simple CSS fade. Journey warm wood with analog-film photography, ONE dominant image. Expertise wine stage, ivory/champagne type with intentional stillness and seven selective reveals. Contact an ivory printed colophon with ONE final visual line echoing the initial act. Rhythm DARK → WARM → DARK WINE → LIGHT; no more six beige pages.

### 3. Expertise is a bespoke TYPOGRAPHIC PERFORMANCE, not a menu
Seven terms all fit at 320px. One 100%-contrast active term; others recede but remain readable. Counter, term highlight and context change under ONE GSAP progress scalar; no independent timers or competing autoAlpha. Correct optical alignment. Remove giant isolated 24vw numeral. Last term receives full reading HOLD before manifesto paper enters, reverse restores all terms. Single editorial champagne reading rule, no hover gimmicks.

### 4. Invisible UX details create visible luxury
iPhone bottom bar and FR+ never cover anything. No clipped letters at 320, clear focus state, reduced-motion all-content mode. Mobile should be designed, not a desktop downgrade. If 1440 has large silence it still needs a compelling focal object. Scroll should remain intuitively usable, audio never mandatory.

### 5. FOUR submission-quality KEYFRAMES WITHOUT MOTION
- Yuna first Work frame: subject dominates the screen, pristine typography and contrast, not a cramped gallery tile.
- Bridge midpoint: leading film frame crosses a precise palette change, not a line of generic cards.
- Journey 2024/25 hero: one powerful photograph and huge year anchored in a crisp margin, meaningful physical continuity.
- Expertise midpoint: editorial wine stage with dramatic readable type, small strong secondary copy, no clipping or ghost numbers.

### 6. Release gate — independent verification
Before/after at 1440x900 and 390x844, plus 320 worst states; exact adjacent seam image/bbox comparisons in forward and reverse; VISUAL examination of actual screenshots; typecheck, ESLint, Webpack build, WebKit/Chromium with no clipped media, nav collisions or page errors; capture forward/reverse video if feasible. If worse than live main, REOPEN. Opus edits only V3; mentor approves publication on main.
